# Aura XLSL Specification

XLSL (Intelligent Spreadsheet Language) defines a workbook-oriented computational format for research, simulation, statistics, AI-assisted analysis, and multidimensional data.

## Core model

A workbook can contain STEM modules, typed ranges, deterministic formulas, datasets, simulation definitions, AI interpretation hooks, provenance records, and extension namespaces.

The semantic workbook is Aura. XLSL is the semantic layer; XLSX may be used for interchange.

## Extensions

- .xlog — experiment/event and provenance logs
- .xsim — simulation definitions and runs
- .xquant — quantitative and quantum-oriented data/state descriptions
- .xdim — multidimensional tensors, spaces, and coordinate metadata
- .xphilo — assumptions, hypotheses, evidence, and research reasoning

## Statistical requirements

Statistical implementations must document risk-set convention, tie handling, censoring semantics, confidence-interval transformation, numerical precision, and comparison against an independent reference implementation.

## Teleportation research

Teleportation entries are conceptual computational models. They are not claims of practical human teleportation. TP-006 is explicitly infeasible with present-day technology.

## Provenance

Research outputs should be attributable to a workbook revision, source dataset, algorithm version, and execution environment.
