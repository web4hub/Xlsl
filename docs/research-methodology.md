# Aura Research Methodology

Aura research workbooks distinguish observations, simulations, hypotheses, and established reference facts.

## Evidence states

- `observed`: directly recorded dataset or measurement.
- `simulated`: produced by a computational model.
- `hypothesis`: proposed explanation or research question.
- `derived`: deterministic transformation of declared inputs.
- `reference`: externally established information cited by the project.

A simulation is never treated as experimental proof by the workbook runtime.

## Reproducibility

Every executable research artifact should record:

- input identifiers or digests
- algorithm/version
- parameters
- random seed when applicable
- execution environment
- outputs
- timestamp
- workbook revision

## Teleportation pipeline

TP-001 through TP-006 remain conceptual research stages. The human-scale stage is explicitly theoretical and currently infeasible. The workbook records state-transition models and feasibility assumptions; it does not provide experimental procedures for human teleportation.
